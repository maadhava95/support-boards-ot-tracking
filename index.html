Attendance Shift In

Record each technician's attendance for a scheduled shift. Each scheduled shift is 12 hours. Five worked shifts = 60 hours. MC, AL or Absent on a scheduled workday records 0 worked hours, leaving room for approved OT to make up missed hours, up to 60 total hours Sunday–Saturday.
